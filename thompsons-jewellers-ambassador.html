FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this an employment position?

Yes. If hired, you will work as a full-time Brand Ambassador, not as an independent contractor.

How is compensation handled?

Compensation is $600 per campaign, with a maximum of four campaigns per week.

What happens after I apply?

Applications are reviewed according to the program requirements. Selected participants receive the applicable campaign information before taking part.

Do I have to pay to apply?

No. No payment or fee is required.
